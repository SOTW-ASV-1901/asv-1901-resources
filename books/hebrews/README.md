# Hebrews | American Standard Version (1901)

**Servants of the Way | Book 58 | additive, independently auditable source-locked foundation**

Hebrews comprises 13 chapters and 303 numbered main-text verses. Original source PDF pages 1229–1240 correspond to printed New Testament pages 235–246. The physical-page index records chapter/verse start and end, including the verses spanning physical page boundaries. Source images, historical layout, typography, and marginal notes are preserved without editing. The original heading identifies the work as “The Epistle to the Hebrews,” without author attribution; the digital library does not introduce an unsupported author attribution.

**Digital text:** The unmodified English-only USX is the primary digital source for the verse body, paragraph starts, and translator-supplied italic spans. The independent-comparison USFM agrees on all numbered verses and supplied spans; however, the independence of its provenance has *not* been established. The original 1901 printing is the historical control for detected disagreements. Original witnesses and the parent source checksum are retained. No unverified word-to-Strong’s alignment is imported.

**Compare original:** Each book has an evaluation HTML reader with chapter/verse selection, compare original, previous/next physical page, and **Next verse page** for page-spanning verses. Original scans are delivered as per-book PDF facsimiles; the 135 MB complete Bible scan is not redistributed.

**Selected manuscript/translation apparatus:** See `data/asv1901/hebrews/textual-apparatus.json` for examples and the original print for all marginal notes. Hebrews 9:4 retains the original 1901 marginal alternative “Or, censer”; the main verse remains unchanged. Original text and historical notes remain distinct.

**About This Translation (planned reader enhancement):** The ASV 1901 Standard American Edition was prepared by the American Revision Committee and historically published by Thomas Nelson & Sons (New York, 1901). Servants of the Way is the digital Bible Translation Library provider, not the original translator or publisher. A shared edition-attribution record and reader panel remain planned; neither has been implemented in this release, and earlier books do not need reprocessing for that feature.

**Validation boundaries:** Local audits cover source SHA-256, chapter/verse positions, supplied italics, original-page navigation, page image streams and placements, rendered-page comparisons, schemas, reader JavaScript and static resources, rebuild reproducibility, and package integrity. These tests do **not** constitute exhaustive character-by-character proofreading of historical print, establish witness independence, or certify hosted browser behavior.
