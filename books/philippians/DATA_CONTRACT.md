# ASV 1901 Philippians book-local data contract

Canonical verse identifiers: `PHP.chapter.verse`; edition identifiers `ASV1901:PHP.chapter.verse`. `spans[].kind` is `text` or `supplied` (translator-supplied italics). Source-page IDs track the original 1901 scanned Bible. The book-specific catalog points to chapter text and original-page facsimiles. Both digital witnesses contain all traditional chapter/verse positions for this book. Word-to-Strong's alignment is not imported without independent verification.
