# ASV 1901 | 2 Chronicles data contract v1.0.0

- A stable `2CH.c.v` identifies Scripture references while `ASV1901:2CH.c.v` identifies the specific ASV rendition. IDs are not URLs.
- **36 chapters / 822 verses** are stored under `data/asv1901/second-chronicles/chapters/`. English text and **118 supplied-word italic spans** remain separate typed `text` / `supplied` segments.
- Historical source-PDF pages **466–502** correspond to printed pages **428–464**. All **37 original Scripture pages** are preserved whole in two smaller PDF volumes.
- **32 verses** span two original printed pages. The index includes both in source order. Original running heads and actual first-verse/page boundaries are stored independently.
- Neither lexical alignments nor Strong's assertions from the comparison witness are verified or imported. Future systems may use the stable Scripture IDs without assuming a Hebrew-to-English word alignment.
- Multiple digital English witnesses are compared, but their genealogical independence is unproven; chapter-level original-print verification is not per-character certification.
