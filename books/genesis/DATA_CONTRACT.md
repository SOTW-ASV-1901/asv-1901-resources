# Genesis data contract 1.0.0

A Scripture reference `GEN.24.67` denotes an identity, not a text rendition, a lexical claim, or a URL. `ASV1901:GEN.24.67` identifies that translation's record. Each chapter file contains `edition_id`, `book_code`, `chapter`, `verses[]`, and `schema_version`. Each verse has `id`, `edition_verse_id`, `verse`, `paragraph_start` and `spans[]` in reading order. A `text` span is regular ASV English; a `supplied` span is printed in italics in this edition and is **not** an independently verified Hebrew/Greek alignment. Preserve the exact English text and punctuation of the spans when projecting other formats. Paragraph and poetry newlines are layout-only information.

Original facsimile pages have stable `source_pdf_page`, a physical-order integer starting at 1, `printed_page` for Scripture pages, `type`, and where appropriate inclusive `first_verse`/`last_verse` boundaries. A verse maps to all source pages containing it, sorted by ascending `source_pdf_page`, with no verse-to-pixel coordinates. Each page also gives a `volume` and a `volume_pdf_page` local 1-based index. Map pages and blank interleaves are historic content, not missing Scripture. Some scanned pages begin with a continuation of the verse shown as the final verse on the prior Scripture page; printed running heads are recorded independently and must not be mistaken for the first physical-page verse. A verse may span successive Scripture pages with maps between them.

The ASV foundation will later publish a versioned cross-foundation resource manifest, but none is falsely asserted present in this Genesis release. Do not reuse these data projections as a Strong's database or assume any Hebrew/Greek words are authenticated by the comparison-only USFM. A future edition may require versification mappings rather than assuming all translations use the same reference granularity. Future schemas must be versioned and migrated without repurposing existing identifiers.
