# 2 Samuel | Historical and technical verification scope

- All **24 chapters / 695 verse records** are present in order. The English-only USX and comparison USFM contain matching English wording after differences in layout whitespace are ignored. This does not establish genealogical independence of the two digital sources.
- All **76 supplied-word italic spans** in the primary source agree with the comparison source and are preserved as typed data.
- The original 1901 scanned edition has **29 contiguous printed Scripture pages**, source PDF pages **338–366** (printed pages 304–332). All 29 page openings and chapter spans have been inspected; 27 verses continue onto the next page. No historical map occurs within these 29 pages.
- Whole original printed pages are preserved in two smaller facsimile PDF volumes with independently tested source-image equivalence: **29/29 embedded image streams and rendered pages matched** the original printing.
- Automated validation checks the schemas, every Scripture reference, source range, source file checksums and ZIP integrity. Independent tests also confirm **identical rebuilt chapter and PDF outputs** and successful retrieval of **eight local HTTP resources**. Independent verification details appear in `VALIDATION_REPORT.json`.
- **Limits:** chapter-level source verification, not character-by-character proofreading of the complete book; the two digital witnesses may have shared origins. The comparison-only Strong's tags are not certified or imported. A hosted browser functional test has not been performed.
