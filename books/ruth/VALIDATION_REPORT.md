# Ruth | Final source and technical validation scope

- Complete book: 4 chapters, 85 verses in canonical order. Both digital witnesses agree in English text after layout-only whitespace normalization. All five translator-supplied italic spans agree exactly between USX and USFM.
- Source-print inspection covers every Ruth Scripture page opening: physical scan pages 294, 297, 298, 299 (printed Bible pages 266–269), and the Canaan map and blank reverse at pages 295–296. Three verses require two-page Compare navigation: RUT.1.22, RUT.2.22, RUT.4.5.
- One small PDF volume contains all six original whole pages, including the map and blank reverse. The source remains unchanged; extracted page image streams and selected rendered pages have been checked for fidelity.
- All chapter JSON files, Scripture verse IDs, historical page index, SHA-256 manifest, ZIP integrity, file paths and reader syntax were validated. The data and PDFs were rebuilt from source with reproducible outputs.
- Limits: Historical check is chapter-level and focused on physical page evidence and differences, **not** exhaustive independent character-by-character proofreading. Digital text witnesses may share upstream ancestry. Strong's tags are not certified. Hosted interactive browser acceptance is not claimed.
