# ASV 1901 | 2 Kings data contract v1.0.0

- `2KI.c.v` is a stable reference; `ASV1901:2KI.c.v` identifies that verse in this edition. IDs are independent of GitHub URLs.
- `data/asv1901/second-kings/chapters/01.json` through `25.json` contain all **719 verse records**, with **67 translator-supplied italic spans** represented as `kind: supplied` and ordinary text as `kind: text`.
- Original source PDF pages **400–435** cover printed 2 Kings pages **366–397** and preserve **32 Scripture pages plus two historical maps and their blank reverse pages**.
- **27 verses** span adjacent Scripture pages. `2KI.16.13` spans source pages **420 and 423**, with an interleaved map and blank reverse; `2KI.23.15` spans **431–432** despite a page-431 running head ending at 23:13.
- Physical page boundaries are based on the text actually printed, not merely the running header. Full original physical pages are kept in two small PDF volumes. No verse pixel locations or reconstructed facsimile typography are asserted.
- The original print was examined at chapter and page-boundary level, but these checks are **not exhaustive character-by-character visual proofreading**.
- Digital source agreement does not prove transcription independence. Unverified experimental Strong's word tags are excluded; original-language relationships require separate verified evidence and contracts.
