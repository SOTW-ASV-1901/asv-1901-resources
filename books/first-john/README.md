# 1 John | American Standard Version (1901)

**Servants of the Way | Book 62 | Additive, source-locked foundation**

1 John comprises 5 chapters and 105 main-text verses. Original PDF pages **1253–1256** correspond to New Testament printed pages **259–262**. The preserved complete original printed pages include the margins, notes, text and physical page transitions.

**Digital witnesses:** The original English-only USX is the main edition witness for Scripture text, chapter/verse positions and translator-supplied italics; the separate USFM is a comparison witness whose independence is not established. Both exact upstream files are preserved unchanged. There is no imported unverified Strong's or original-language word alignment.

**Compare original:** This book includes its own chapter/verse reader, facsimile viewer, Previous/Next page controls and Next verse page control for physical page-spanning verses. These files are designed to run from repository-root GitHub Pages. The complete historical 135 MB parent source scan is SHA-pinned but excluded from the ZIP.

**Historic apparatus:** 1 John 4:3 is printed in the main text with a marginal manuscript alternative, “Some ancient authorities read annulleth Jesus.” The alternative must remain a note, not overwrite the printed verse. The separately recorded selected notes are at `data/asv1901/first-john/textual-apparatus.json`; the complete historic printed notes remain in the scanned facsimile. The main Scripture reading follows the historical 1901 text, not later translations.

**About This Translation (future enhancement, not yet implemented):** The ASV 1901 Standard American Edition was prepared by the American Revision Committee and historically published by Thomas Nelson & Sons (New York, 1901). Servants of the Way is the digital Bible Translation Library provider, not the original translator or historical publisher. A shared edition-level attribution record and reader-facing panel are planned, without rebuilding the previously accepted book records.

**Verification and limits:** The deterministic builder checks original source hashes, traditional verse IDs and comparison witness text and italics. Independent checks cover facsimile image identity, physical verse page coverage, schemas, reader JS syntax, eight local HTTP resources, archival hashes and reproducibility. This is **not** exhaustive character-by-character printed-text proofreading, proof of independent digital witnesses, or hosted-browser certification.
