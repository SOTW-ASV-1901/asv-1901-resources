# ASV 1901 Ecclesiastes | Independent validation report

This release contains **12 chapters, 222 verses, and 55 structured translator-supplied-word italic spans**. Both digital witnesses agree in every verse after whitespace/layout normalization, and the 55 supplied-word spans agree verse by verse. The source witnesses have not been established as independent.

All nine original 1901 Scripture pages (source PDF 702–710, printed 660–668) were visually checked at their openings/closings against the scan. Their original full-page imagery is preserved as one compact facsimile volume. Embedded image digests/layout and 1× rendered page pixels were checked against the source for all nine pages. Every verse maps to its original page; seven verses span adjacent pages: 2:7, 3:8, 4:16, 6:8, 7:27, 9:4, and 10:17.

The independent checks include source word agreement, italic spans, chapter JSON/page-index schemas, SHA-256 manifests, exact PDF preservation, deterministic rebuilds, reader JavaScript syntax, local HTTP resource retrieval, and ZIP integrity. No original text, language word alignments, Strong’s assignments, external commentaries, or new shared indexes were inferred.

**Scope limitations:** Physical printing was not collated letter-for-letter; the original USX and comparison USFM may have shared lineage; browser hosting and UI interaction have not been tested. The complete original scanned PDF is checksum-locked outside the public ZIP, with original digital subfiles unchanged inside the ZIP. The release contains only Ecclesiastes-specific paths and its per-book catalog.
