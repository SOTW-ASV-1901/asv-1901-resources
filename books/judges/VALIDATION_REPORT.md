# Judges verification scope

All 21 Judges chapters and 618 verse records are present and numbered correctly. The English-only USX and comparison USFM agree in every verse after layout whitespace differences are ignored, and their 62 translator-supplied italic spans agree exactly. They may share upstream ancestry: this is not a claim of fully independent textual witnesses.

Original printed Scripture pages 239–265, occupying source PDF pages 267–293, were examined for actual first-verse boundary, including verse continuations ignored by running heads. The page index covers 618/618 verses, including 25 two-page verses. Two smaller PDF volumes preserve every whole historical source page. No historical maps occur inside this specific Judges page range.

The release passed structural, schema, image-source-preservation, reproducibility, hash, ZIP-integrity, relative-link, and no-collision checks. Source scope is chapter-level comparison against the original scan, not exhaustive character-by-character visual certification. No Strong's mappings were imported as verified, and hosted interactive browser acceptance remains untested.
