# ASV 1901 | Joshua

One-book independently installable resource release for the SOTW ASV 1901 Foundation.

- 24 chapters, 658 verses, 43 supplied-word italic spans, 26 original Scripture pages and 2 historical map-related leaves.
- 23 page-spanning verses preserved in correct original-source order.
- Paths are additive only: `books/joshua/`, `data/asv1901/joshua/`, and `data/asv1901/catalog-joshua.json`.
- A per-book inspection reader is included for local/hosted quality assurance. The central Servants of the Way website remains a separate application, not installed by this book.
- Do not upload the ZIP as a repository file; extract it and upload its `books/` and `data/` directories to repository root, preserving their contained paths.
- No master catalog, central UI, or other ASV book files are included or replaced.
