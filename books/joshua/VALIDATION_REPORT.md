# Joshua final verification scope

All 24 Joshua chapters and 658 verse records are present, ordered, and compared between the primary English-only USX and comparison-only USFM. Their English wording agrees at every verse when whitespace used for formatting is ignored; 43 supplied-word italic spans also agree exactly. These two witnesses may derive from related editorial sources, so their agreement does not prove independent textual ancestry.

Physical 1901 page openings were checked and mapped for all 26 Scripture pages (original PDF 239–266, omitting map and blank at 245–246). 23 verses continue onto a second Scripture page, including JOS.7.13 spanning PDF pages 244 and 247 around the map insertion. Whole historical pages and map leaves are preserved without rasterizing them.

Validation includes complete chapter/verse coverage, schema validation, Source-to-PDF image stream parity, integrity of relative links, source-file SHA-256 inventories, reproducible build checks, ZIP CRC and hash verification, and collision checks against earlier releases.

**Limits:** The chapter-level original-print check is not exhaustive character-by-character proofreading of all verses. Interactive testing on a hosted public GitHub Pages site is outstanding. Strong's word alignment from the USFM is not certified or imported.
