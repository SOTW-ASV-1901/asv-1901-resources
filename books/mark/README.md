# Mark | American Standard Version (1901)

**Servants of the Way | Book 41 | standalone, additive, source-locked foundation**

The Mark edition contains **16 chapters**, **673 actual ASV 1901 main-text verses**, 678 traditional verse positions, 1 historical Gospel map and 22 original printed Scripture pages. It retains the exact original PDF pages **1012–1034**, printed New Testament pages **35–56** for Scripture. The whole 1901 parent scan is preserved outside the ZIP, with its SHA-256 hash and full source identity.

## Print textual apparatus is not main Scripture text

The printed original includes notes for five verse numbers omitted from running Scripture text: **Mark 7:16, 9:44, 9:46, 11:26, and 15:28**. The comparison USFM witness includes all five as normal verse text, but this original 1901 publication does not. They are documented under `data/asv1901/mark/verse-omissions.json`, not invented as main-text verses. **Mark 16:9–20 appears in the original main text**, but the printed apparatus says the two oldest Greek manuscripts and some other authorities omit from verse 9 to the end; some other authorities have a different ending. Its notice is documented in `data/asv1901/mark/textual-apparatus.json` and surfaced in the reader.

## Sources and text preservation

Primary source: original 1901 printed Bible. Exact English-only USX file is preserved for structured verse text and italics. Exact eBible USFM is preserved as comparison only. **17 detected differences** are typographic (ligatures, diacritic, or historical capitalization). Both upstream files are byte-preserved. The print-backed small-capital inscription in Mark 15:26 is represented in uppercase in derived plain text, with the change documented in the comparison record. Source witness independence is not established. The independently printed evidence must be consulted for edition appearance; this release does not claim exhaustive character-by-character proofreading against every printed verse. Neither input's English-to-Strong's tags are assumed to be independently verified original-language alignments.

Original page openings, endings and continuations are indexed so each verse can find its actual scanned page(s). Historical maps and notes remain physically intact. Validation details are in `VALIDATION_REPORT.*`, and SHA-256 manifest covers the complete standalone release.
