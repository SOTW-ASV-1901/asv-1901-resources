# 1 Samuel | Historical and technical validation record

- Complete **31 chapters and 810 unique verses** in canonical order. Primary USX and comparison USFM have identical English wording after layout spacing normalization except two documented, source-checked differences: 13:1 `[forty]` and 14:49 `Malchi-shua`.
- **80 translator-supplied italic spans** exactly agree in both digital sources. Italics are stored in `spans` as structured `kind: supplied` information.
- Original print PDF pages **300–337** contain **34 Scripture-bearing pages**, two historical map insertions and two blank reverse pages. Page openings, chapter transitions and continuation boundaries checked. **32 verses** span two Scripture pages, including cases across historical map insertions.
- Whole historical PDF leaves retained in **two smaller PDFs** without independent rasterization.
- Chapter JSON schema, page index, source hashes and release manifest support automated revalidation.
- **Scope:** chapter-level inspection and source-variant reconciliation, **not** character-by-character proofreading. Digital witness independence is not established; Strong's and original-language mappings are not certified. Hosted interactive browser testing remains to be performed.
