# 1 Samuel | ASV 1901 Scripture Foundation

This independent installation includes **31 chapters / 810 verses**, structured italics, full 1901 scanned-page navigation, original historical maps, source comparison evidence and a separate inspection reader.

- Canonical data: `data/asv1901/first-samuel/`
- Provenance and validations: `books/first-samuel/`
- Temporary inspection interface: `books/first-samuel/site/index.html`
- Independent validation: `python3 books/first-samuel/scripts/validate_release.py .`

Research and archive inputs are kept separate from the authoritative derived edition data. The official multi-edition Bible reader will be hosted elsewhere, in the central Servants of the Way organization.
