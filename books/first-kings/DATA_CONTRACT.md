# ASV 1901 | 1 Kings data contract v1.0.0

- `1KI.c.v` is the stable Scripture verse identity; `ASV1901:1KI.c.v` is the edition-specific verse identity. Neither depends upon URLs or storage hosts.
- `data/asv1901/first-kings/chapters/01.json` through `22.json` include all **816 verse records**. Their **84 italic spans** are `kind: supplied`; ordinary spans are `kind: text`.
- Source PDF pages **367–399** are printed Scripture pages **333–365**. All **33 original physical pages** are preserved whole in two PDF volumes, without retyping or destructive rasterization.
- **27 verses** continue onto two original pages, including `1KI.18.32` on PDF pages 392–393. Physical `first_verse` may precede the page's printed `running_head_first_verse`; original ordering governs the navigation index.
- Original page openings and chapter page coverage were visually checked; no pixel coordinates, word-level facsimile mapping, or exhaustive character-by-character visual proof is claimed.
- The separate digital witnesses agree on all 816 verses after whitespace differences are ignored. Their genealogical independence is **not established**. Their italic content also agrees, but this is not an exhaustive visual proof of every italic in the original scan.
- Experimental Strong's tagging from comparison USFM is **not** authenticated or included. Language and commentary resources will require separate source-validated contracts.
