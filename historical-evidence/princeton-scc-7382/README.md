## Purpose and scope

This record preserves public catalog evidence associated with a particular printed American Standard Version Bible held by **Princeton Theological Seminary Library, Special Collections**. It supports the shared Servants of the Way Scripture History Library. It is **not** a new transcription, replacement facsimile, provenance certificate, or change to the verse-to-print-page reader.

The original email from the archivist is **not included** in this public package; it is retained separately by the project owner. This file records only an attributed summary of the supplied correspondence.

## Institutional inquiry and limits of the record

In a reply presented to Servants of the Way on November 2, 2026, **Brian Shetler, Head of Special Collections and Archives at Princeton Theological Seminary**, stated that the library could find no specific provenance beyond the physical volume and catalog entry. Based on the flyleaf inscription, he considered acquisition by the Seminary in **1930 or 1931** plausible, following the committee meeting held in New York City in April 1930.

This is an **informed interpretation of the inscription**, not a verified accession ledger date. The institution did **not** identify or independently authenticate all autograph signers, determine when they signed, or establish that the book was present at the April 1930 meeting. The catalog note does not by itself identify which editorial committee signed the book.

The original correspondence remains privately held by the owner and should not be uploaded to a public repository without a separate publication decision.

## Evidence classification

- **Documented from institution's catalog:** record ID, call number, 1901 printing details, physical description, and committee-autograph local note.
- **Archivist's qualified assessment:** possible Seminary acquisition circa 1930–1931 based on handwritten inscription.
- **Historically established but not physically linked:** an American Bible revision committee met in New York in April 1930.
- **Unverified in this record:** named autograph signers, their identities and authentication, the signing circumstances, and the volume's full ownership chain.
